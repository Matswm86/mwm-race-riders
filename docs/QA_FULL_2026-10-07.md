# QA: MWM Race Riders full game (2026-10-07)

**Verdict: NEEDS-CHANGES.** Nothing blocks a sideload. Lint, load, the race test (390 PASS, 0 fail), CI and every screen pass. A non-reading child who never steers finishes all 96 races, and with one tap per screen climbs the Bronze III season to Bronze II. Five things keep this from SHIP:
- The pre-race hand hint reads as a raised middle finger.
- World 6 is close to a black frame, and the child's own rider is invisible against the asphalt (contrast 1.02:1).
- One tap on "replay" traps a child who follows the biggest disc in free rides of the same track, so the league never moves on.
- Real races go over the draw limits (Høy 82-85, Lav 61-64). The budget phase samples 6 points per track and misses these peaks.
- The triangle counts are far over the DESIGN 12 ceilings, worst on World 6 and on the Lav tier the 32-bit tablet runs.

Audited commit: `387616f` (main = HEAD, working tree clean). The Android build and gdlint for it are green, and the release asset was updated after that build.

## 6. Findings, ranked (fix in this order)

1. **High (child and parent):** `assets/textures/ui/hud_atlas.png` sprite "hand" (`scripts/RrHudAtlas.gd:34`, made by `tools/art/make_hud_atlas.py`). The hint hand reads as a raised middle finger: one centred finger, no thumb, no knuckles. Every first launch shows two of them (shots 01, 02). Redraw it as a tapping index hand: finger off-centre, a thumb, curled knuckle lines, tilted 15-20 degrees.
2. **High (child UX, look):** World 6 lighting (`scripts/RrWorld.gd` world-6 look, DESIGN 11.4/11.5). 62-71% of the frame is below L 0.01 (sRGB mean 19-25, against 68-142 for the other worlds), and the player is at 1.02:1 against the asphalt. Raise `tonemap_exposure` and ambient light, and lighten the wet asphalt albedo. Add the per-racer light pool that DESIGN 11.5 already prescribes, plus a rim light or brighter jersey emission on the player. Aim for the player at 3:1 or better against the ground, and for no more than 20% of pixels below L 0.01.
3. **Medium (child UX):** `scripts/RrMain.gd:447-449` (`_on_card_replay` starts a free ride) with `scripts/RrCard.gd`. After a free ride the biggest disc is replay, so a child loops on one track and the league never moves on. When the free ride started from a league card and the season is still open, keep the league "next" disc on the free-ride card as the biggest disc. Or make replay from a league card a league re-race, or swap replay and next so the middle slot is "next".
4. **Medium (perf):** the draw limits are broken in real races: Høy 82-85, Lav 61-64. Make `tests/capture.gd` `_phase_budget` record the per-frame maximum over a whole race (as `track_probe.gd` does), not 6 teleport points. Then cut 3-6 draws: merge ground chunks (12-17 draws), drop the racer shadow pass for rivals beyond 15 m, and count the ghost in the budget.
5. **Medium (perf, 32-bit tablet):** triangles are over the DESIGN 12 ceilings: Lav 104-179k against 90k on every world, Høy W6 311-317k against 200k. On W6 the near container LOD and the far stacks are the first suspects (DESIGN 12a estimated 88k Lav). Decide whether the ceilings or the content change. Without a tablet reading this is the biggest unknown.
6. **Medium (size, future Play):** the APK is 294.6 MB. The native libs take 145 MB stored uncompressed (`compress_native_libraries=false`). The music is 43 MB because the track is still 92.6 min (old finding 8). Trimming the music to the GDD's 18.7 min saves about 33 MB. For sideload, compressing the native libs cuts the download substantially; for Play, an AAB split per ABI is the route. Re-check Play's download-size limit before a store build.
7. **Low (shell integration):** `scripts/RrMain.gd:350-353` / `scripts/RrState.gd:268-275`. `free_levels_finished` fires as the season card opens. Emit it when the child taps the season card's disc, so the shell card follows the podium instead of covering it.
8. **Low (look):** W5 shows 15-25 px pixel blocks in the sky or backdrop seen through canopy gaps (`worlds/20_w5_split_path.png`, top right; crop `w5_crop.png`). The 1k panorama is magnified there; use a 2k sky or more canopy cover.
9. **Low (look):** league table and board. The helmet badge covers half of two-digit jersey numbers ("12", "10", "11" in shots 08 and 14). Move the badge further out or shrink the digits.
10. **Low (design):** a skilled Vanlig bot wins by 1.5 s or more in only 2 of 10 races in W5 and W6 (6 of 10 in W1-W4; GDD hint 9). This is the game designer's call.
11. **Info:** old findings 9 (vibration row) and 13 (save name `race_riders_save.json` against the GDD's `mwm_race_riders_save.json`) are still open. Pick a save name before the MWM Play adapter exists.
12. **Info:** leak warnings at quit (2-18 resources, and 1347 dummy mesh RIDs in the race test) in every run. They are harmless, but they hide real leaks.
