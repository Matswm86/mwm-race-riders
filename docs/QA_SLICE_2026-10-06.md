# QA: MWM Race Riders vertical slice (2026-10-06)

**Verdict: NEEDS-CHANGES.** Nothing blocks a sideload. Lint, load, the race test, CI and every screen pass. A non-reading 4-year-old who touches nothing still finishes in 44 s, usually 2nd, and reaches the card. Real touch events confirm the boost disc never steers. Four things keep this from SHIP:
- In race 2 the ghost sits on top of the child's own rider for 40% of the race.
- The draw counts are over both phone budgets.
- The boost disc shows "active" before GO.
- There is no phone performance reading.

Audited commit: `5bcc4e2` (main = HEAD; CI build and gdlint green for it). Per the coordinator's update, the owner rejected the toy art style, so the "does Høy read premium" verdict and the three visual upgrades are left out. Everything below covers code that stays.

## 3. MWM Play fit and the `set_full_unlock(false)` path (exercised)

With `Engine.set_meta(&"mwm_play_shell", true)`, `set_full_unlock(false)`, `set_sfx_on(false)` and `set_music_on(false)`:
```
shell: home visible false gear visible true full_unlock false
[race pre] / [card] / [settings] / [page] tappable in top-left 232 or y>=1664: []
finish 1: free_signals 0 card_signals 1   (hover reveal shown)
finish 2: free_signals 0 card_signals 2
finish 3: free_signals 0 -> after replay tap: free_signals 1
finish 4: free_signals 1 -> after replay tap: free_signals 1   (once per session)
shell settings rows: Innstillinger, Lyd, slider, slider, Spøkelse/På, Grafikk/Høy
shell back: screen race -> race (left to the shell)
```
- **Free card:** it fires once, on the first card close from total finish 3 on, as GDD 9.2 says. The track page shows track 1 only.
- **Audio:** the game forces `sfx.enabled`/`music.enabled` true in the shell (`RrMain.gd:125,127`) and relies on the shell muting its `Sfx`/`Music` buses. That works because the shell reroutes every player by stream path (`ShellAdapter.bus_for`), and RrMusic sets its stream before `add_child`. Nothing to fix, but there is no adapter yet to prove it end to end.
- **Shell signal:** the shell has no `free_levels_finished` handler for any game yet (`grep` in `mwm-play/app/shell` finds none). The signal fires on the replay or home tap, and `start_race()` runs in the same frame. Unless the shell pauses the tree, the pre-race auto-start (4 s) runs behind its card. This is for the adapter author.
- **Settings in the shell:** the shell settings panel keeps the gaps of the hidden rows (s_settings: empty space at y 340-530 and 1140-1270).

## 4. Performance risk (static plus laptop probe; no phone attached)

| Item | Measured | Budget (DESIGN 7e) |
|---|---|---|
| Draws, Høy, race | 82 (s 100, shots), 86 (cost probe) | phone <= 80: **over** |
| Draws, Lav, race | 67 (s 100), 70 (skyway) | tablet <= 60: **over** |
| HUD share | 16 draws (67 -> 51 with the HUD hidden) | about 8 in the DESIGN table |
| Triangles | Høy 90.8k, Lav 66.4k | 100k / 70k: OK, Lav is close |
| Shadows (Høy) | +19 draws, +24k tris, +0.96 ms on the 3060 | design says cut; owner chose Høy |
| Lights / particles / textures | 1 directional; GPU particle pool small; largest texture 512 (icon), palette 64 px | OK |

- **Per-frame allocations:** `RrHud._process` calls `queue_redraw()` every frame (`RrHud.gd:181`), and `_draw_bar` builds 2 `StyleBoxFlat.new()` per frame (`RrHud.gd:215,224`), plus a third during the lights (`:276`). In Vanlig the clock string is formatted every frame (`:193`). The whole HUD re-tessellates anti-aliased arcs every frame, which is likely why it costs 16 draws. The boost trail rebuilds an ImmediateMesh each frame while boosting (`RrWorld._update_trail`). `RrMain._process` and `RrGhost.sample` create small arrays each frame. Nothing is large, but the HUD is the one to fix.
- **Laptop GPU time:** 5.9 ms (Høy) and 3.8 ms (Lav) on an RTX 3060 at 1080x1920 is high for this scene. It says nothing exact about a phone, but it points to fill or overdraw beyond the draw count. Lav with the HUD hidden still costs 3.6 ms.
- **Music:** `race_riders_theme.ogg` is 42.2 MB, Vorbis 64 kbps stereo, and **92.6 min** long (full decode: `time=01:32:35`). GDD 11 (`docs/GDD.md:436`) says 18.7 min. It streams (`oggvorbisstr`), so RAM is fine, but it is about 20% of the 206.9 MB APK. Siblings are 153-182 MB.
- **32-bit tablet:** it starts on Lav (`RrState.gd:34`). The auto-drop works and is saved (probe). The drop is permanent until a parent switches back.
- **PerfOverlay:** the autoload is present and toggles on a three-finger tap. **Phone performance: UNVERIFIED.** It needs fps, draws and VRAM from the phone (Høy) and from the tablet (Lav) at s 100 and on the skyway.

## 6. Findings

1. **Medium (child UX):** `scripts/RrWorld.gd:625-650`, `scripts/RrMain.gd:416-420`. The ghost replays the same kid line as an idle child and covers the player for 40% of race 2 (08, 09, 10). The child loses their own rider. Hide or fade the ghost while it is within about 3 m of the player, or offset it laterally (and draw it behind the player).
2. **Medium (perf):** both draw counts are over budget: Høy 82-86 vs 80, Lav 67-70 vs 60. The HUD alone costs 16. Bake the static HUD parts (bar track, boost ring background) into textures or a single `draw_*` batch, and stop redrawing the HUD every frame when nothing changed (`scripts/RrHud.gd:181,215,224`). Shadows add 19 draws in Høy.
3. **Low-Medium (bug):** `scripts/RrRider.gd:36,63-64` with `scripts/RrRace.gd:79,89`. Before GO, `race.t` is -1.2, which is below the default `boost_until` of -1.0, so `boosting()` is true. The boost disc draws as active (sun-yellow) in the pre-race (01) and the trail code runs. Initialise `boost_until` to -99, or check `phase == RACE` in `boosting`.
4. **Low-Medium (safe area):** `scripts/RrHud.gd:202-211`. The place medal sits at (540, 112) and spans y 10-260 at top centre (DESIGN 4, against GDD 10.1 `docs/GDD.md:321`, which puts it at (540, 350)). Nothing moves it for the safe area, so on punch-hole phones the camera hole lands on the medal. With a 120 px notch, the lowered home disc also runs into the progress-bar token (probe safe_notch120). Shift the medal and bar by the safe-area dy, or follow the GDD placement.
5. **Low (touch):** `scripts/RrMain.gd:321-323`. The "never steers" corner uses a fixed y < 232. With a notch, the home and gear hit rects grow to 216 + dy, so a touch at (104, 248) both taps home and steers (probe). Use `HOME_HIT + dy` there.
6. **Low (child rule 6):** `scripts/RrCard.gd:101-112`. The reveal card continues on a release anywhere, including the wrist strip (a tap at y 1800 started race 2). Ignore releases at y >= 1664 on the reveal.
7. **Low (child UX):** `scripts/RrCard.gd`. The card has no idle cue. Add a 1 Hz pulse on the replay disc after 6-8 s (rule 18), as the race already does.
8. **Low (size):** `assets/music/race_riders_theme.ogg` is 92.6 min, 42 MB. Trim it to the 18.7 min the GDD describes (about 9 MB at 64 kbps) or ask the owner. Fix GDD 11 either way.
9. **Low (spec drift):** `scripts/RrSettings.gd` has no Vibration row, and `RrState.set_haptics_on` (`scripts/RrState.gd:115`) has no effect. Build it or update GDD 10.7 and 11.
10. **Low (shell look):** `scripts/RrSettings.gd:48-96`. In shell mode the hidden rows leave two empty bands in the panel. Re-flow the rows when `in_shell()`.
11. **Low (test):** `tests/race_test.gd:159-176`. The touch-zone checks ran on a 1920x1920 headless viewport, not portrait. Pass `--resolution 1080x1920` in the README command, or assert the size.
12. **Low (design):** a skilled Vanlig bot wins by >= 1.5 s in 6 of 10 races (GDD 14 hint: 9 of 10). Game designer's call.
13. **Info:** the save file is `user://race_riders_save.json` (`scripts/RrState.gd:17`), but the GDD says `user://mwm_race_riders_save.json`. Pick one before the MWM Play adapter exists.
14. **Info:** the 20:9 intro camera clips through the player's helmet about 1 s after launch (probe safe_tall2400). It is transient and gone by 1.5 s.
15. **Info (integration):** the MWM Play shell does not handle `free_levels_finished` yet, and the game starts the next pre-race in the same frame it emits the signal (section 3).
16. **Info:** leak warnings at quit (2-10 resources) in the load check, the race test and the bot. These are harmless, but they hide real leaks.
